- **`core/`** — pure logic, **no Nodes** (all `RefCounted`/static): `DiceScoring` (Balatro-style hand values = base points × multiplier), `CharmEffects`/`MaterialEffects`/`EssenceEffects`/`RuneEffects` (id-dispatched effect resolution; `EtchingEffects` is down to the single `MIN_FACE_VALUE` since the six number engravings died with the Serienschaltung), `SeriesResolver` (the workshop's whole calculation, see *Die SERIENSCHALTUNG*), `GameRun` (the persistent run state — money, 30-die pool, charms, engravings, sealed packs (`owned_packs` — array order IS the Magazin order, and since 2026-10-28 it is ALWAYS sorted by type (Spieler-Wunsch): `_stash_pack` sorts after every insert through `_type_key` = `Pack.SHELF_ORDER` rank, content id, and for material/rune cards the most frequent material/rune of the net (`_dominant_id`), then uid; `next_pack_spot` predicts the sorted place, and the Verdichtung re-sorts its Rohling. **Das SORTEN-MODELL** (Welle 29, 2026-10-23; es ersetzt das REIHEN-MODELL samt `Pack.shelf_row`, `reorder_packs`, `place_pack`, `ensure_pack_rows`, `set_pack_grid`/`set_pack_capacity`, `pack_capacity`, `PACK_ROWS` und `PACK_CAPACITY`): das Magazin ist UNBEGRENZT, und je `Pack.SHELF_ORDER`-Sorte gibt es EINE Reihe des Magazins (im Code weiter „tray“) - eine Karte liegt IMMER in der ihrer Sorte (`Pack.shelf_rank`, `pack_tray_of(uid)`), ihr PLATZ ist ihr Rang unter den Paketen DERSELBEN Sorte in Listenordnung (`pack_cell_of`, `pack_tray_count`). Ein Verbrauch entfernt nur aus der Liste - seine Reihe schließt sich von selbst. `_stash_pack` ist der EINE Einlagerungsweg und legt sortiert ein, `next_pack_spot(pack, extra)` sagt Reihe und Platz der nächsten Lieferung DIESES Typs voraus (daran mißt `WorkshopView.arrival_anchor_px(pack)`), `packs_full()` ist immer false und `pack_room()` unbegrenzt - der Fizzle-Weg (`PACK_FIZZLE_MONEY`, `grant_pack` → null) bleibt als toter Rückfall stehen. Umsortieren per Zug gibt es nicht mehr; `tidy_packs` sortiert, und weil die Sorte die Reihe ist, wirkt das nur innerhalb jeder Sorte. Spalte und Roll-Stand sind ANZEIGE und wohnen im Fenster (`PackDrawerView`, `WorkshopView`), round progress; owns all economy methods and emits signals to the UI. Charms are hard-capped at `CHARM_CAPACITY` (6, the table's `CharmRowView.SPOT_COUNT`): `_grant_charm` refuses when `charms_full()`, `purchase_charm` therefore checks **before** paying, and the shop/Schwarzmarkt charm cards lock themselves instead of queueing an invisible seventh charm), `ScoreBreakdown`, `SlotMachine` (the Fumble-Automaten: a 5×9 symbol wall whose runs of 3+ pay sealed goods. A Gravur-Reihe's **length decides both the count and the Paketgröße**, and that ladder is `PACK_LADDER` — 3 → 1 Standard, 4 → 1 Groß, 5 → 1 Kolossal, and from `KOLOSSAL_FROM` (5) each further length lifts by one Kolossal, 6 → 2, 7 → 3, … 9 → 5 (`TOTAL_COLS` is the ceiling). The ladder is **uniform across the three engraving sorts** — the old per-sort yield split (Zahlen `length−2`, Material/Würfel-Gravur `length−3`) is gone, because the scarcity already lives in `_symbol_table`'s weights: a rare sort's run is *rarer*, and paying it less on top double-counted that. The run's symbol still decides WHICH sort is minted. `pack_payout(length)` is the one read of the table, the spec dict carries `tier` next to `count`, `SlotPrize.from_spec` mints through `Pack.tiered` (so name decoration and price scaling come free) and `pot_summary()["packs"]` groups the pot by sort **and** size so the display can name it — the old flat `engravings`/`materials`/`edges` counts stay for every caller that only wants a number. The ladder now grows strictly monotonically — the old deliberate dent (6 out-yielding 7) died with the shorter Standard/Groß rungs, and a test nails the monotonic growth. **Das seltene Symbol der höheren Automaten ist ⚡, nicht mehr der Charm** (2026-08-28): `SlotPrize.Kind.ENERGY` ersetzt `CHARM` restlos (Feld `charm` und `_roll_charm` sind tot, `from_spec` braucht darum weder `owned_essences` noch `features` mehr), und seine Leiter ist `ENERGY_LADDER` — 3 → 1⚡, 4 → 2⚡, 5 → 3⚡, ab 6 fest `ENERGY_MAX` (4). Sie ist bewußt FLACH: ein Dreh kostet 1⚡, die Mindestreihe zahlt ihn also genau zurück. `energy_payout(length)` ist der EINE Lesezugriff (Spiegel von `pack_payout`), und `pot_summary()["energy"]` ist die Summe statt der alten `charms`-Liste), `SideBet` (the Nebenwetten catalog: every template carries an `unlock` Fortschrittsstufe, so the shelf grows with the run — `roll_offers(count, tier, quota, stock)` filters the pool *before* shuffling; `UNLOCK_BASE` is 1, so side bets lie out from round 1. **`stock` ist die Inventar-Sicht des Magazins als reine Daten** (`GameRun.pack_stock()` → `SideBet.pack_stock_key(type, tier)` → Anzahl; core/ fasst kein Lager an, es bekommt eine Zählung): eine Vorlage mit **Paket-Einsatz** zieht Sorte und Größe daraus (`_name_stake_packs`, sortierte Kandidaten) statt sie frei zu würfeln, und ohne passenden Besitz **liegt sie gar nicht aus** — die nächste Vorlage rückt nach, die Auslage bleibt voll. Verzehrt wird dann genau die genannte Ware: `GameRun.stake_packs_for(bet)` ist die EINE Auswahl (die letzten passenden, typ- und größengenau), `place_side_bet` gibt sie an `_consume_packs(packs)` und `can_place_side_bet` prüft sie — nicht mehr die Regalgröße. **Der GEWINNWERT** (Spieler-Entscheid 2026-11-10, Obsidian-Seite 42): keine Vorlage trägt mehr einen festen Gewinn, nur ihre Quote (`odds`: `ODDS_EASY` ×2 / `ODDS_MEDIUM` ×2,5 / `ODDS_HARD` ×3). `stake_value()` ist der UNBESTEUERTE Einsatz in $ (Geld wie gedruckt, Pakete zum Ladenpreis `Pack.tier_price(Pack.type_price(t), tier)`, Steuer je Hand ×4, je Würfel ×10, ⚡ ×10), `payout_value(factor)` = Einsatzwert × Quote × factor, als Ware ×`WARE_BONUS` 1,25, gerundet. `roll_reward()` würfelt beim Auslegen die WARE aus `WARES` (Gewicht und Mindestwert; Geld prüft ohne, Ware mit ×1,25) — eine Paket-Einsatz-Vorlage würfelt nach `_name_stake_packs` neu, denn erst dann steht ihr Wert. Die Arten: Geld, Regal-Karten (`Payout.PACKS` + `reward_pack_type`), `MULTIPLIER`, `DIE`, `PART` (ein Laden-Modul ≤ Wert), `ENERGY` (Wert / `ENERGY_PRICE` 8), `CATALYST` (`BET_CATALYSTS`, nur die Treibladung), `OPERATOR`, `PACK` (ein `Pack.roll_special_pack()`); Platinenauftrag und Reinraum behalten ihren festen `Payout.SPECIAL`. Karten: größte Größe, deren Preis ≤ Wert, Anzahl Wert / Preis, höchstens `MAX_REWARD_CARDS` 4 (`reward_tier`/`reward_count`). Der WÜRFEL (`reward_die`, `DiceOffer.roll_bet_die`) trägt seine Seele nach dem Wert (`bet_soul_rarity`: < $12 höchstens gewöhnlich, ab 12 selten, 20 episch, 32 legendär, eine Stufe tiefer, falls der Topf leer ist) und geht den Weg des Ladenkaufs (`stash_die` → `pending_dice` → ZWANG; in der Auszahlungs-Phase ist der Vorrat gesperrt, also zieht der Zwang erst im Laden auf — wie bei der Boss-Prämie). `reward_label`/`stake_label` nennen alles über `Pack.amount_phrase`/`named_phrase`. Point targets are not authored, they are **scaled**: a `target_factor` times the **stored** `run.round_goal`, rounded by `_nice_target` and **frozen into the offer** at roll time — deliberately not `effective_goal()`, so a quota clause signed after the betting window can never move a number already printed on a button. Two stake kinds cost **nothing at placement** and bill per taken hand/die instead (`MONEY_PER_HAND`/`MONEY_PER_DIE`, collected by `GameRun.tax_side_bets` next to the clause fee in the take); if the money no longer covers the bill the bet is `voided` — it stops taxing and loses, which is the whole risk of a tax bet. `GameRun.side_bet_payout_factor()` (Quotenbonus, Hoher Tisch) multipliziert den GEWINNWERT — auch ein Einzelstück wird dadurch besser (größere Karte, seltenere Seele); `side_bet_stake_factor()` (Wettsteuer, Hoher Tisch) ist ein Float, gerundet wird aufwärts (`SideBet.scaled`). Jede Wette merkt, was ihr Einsatz WIRKLICH kostete (`paid_money` samt gezahlter Steuer, `paid_energy`, `paid_packs`) — die Rückversicherung gibt genau das zurück, dieselben Kassetten. **Der Press-Schub ist am 2026-11-07 gestorben** (`Payout.PRESS_BOOST`, `grant_press_boost`, `press_boost_pending`, `SeriesResolver.BOOST_SLOTS` — Spieler-Wunsch): an seine Stelle treten MODUL-Gewinne (`Payout.PART`, Kettenreaktion und Bastler im Grundstock, Prototyp ab Stufe 5, Patent ab 8). **Die Gewinn-KARTEN stehen ab dem Auslegen fest**: `SideBet.reward_cards(factor)` würfelt sie samt Prägenetz je Größe EINMAL, die Grube zeigt genau sie und `_pay_side_bet` gewährt genau sie (`pays_cards()`). An offer the wallet cannot cover shows it: the Setzen button tints `CasinoStyle.RED` — the shop's `price_tint` grammar — via `SideBetPanel._sync_affordability`, re-asked while the window is open through `scene_root._refresh_side_bet_affordability` on money/pack/⚡ changes; the two tax stakes cost nothing at placement and never tint), `DiceOffer` (dice templates + refinement rolls; also `roll_reward_die`, the ONE souled-die roll every Prämie uses), `GameSettings` (die DREI Lautstärken — Master, Musik, Effekte — plus Vollbild in `user://settings.cfg`; `apply()` schreibt sie auf Bus 0, `BUS_MUSIC` und `BUS_SFX`, wobei ein fehlender Bus (−1) still übergangen wird und 0 stummschaltet statt `linear_to_db(0)` = −inf zu setzen — die Ansicht MELDET Änderungen, `scene_root` wendet an und sichert).

### Die PAKETE des Ladens und die AUSWAHL (2026-11-10)
Der Laden verkauft Karten- und Würfel-PAKETE. **Seit dem 2026-11-17 ÖFFNET der Kauf** (Plan `docs/plan_oeffnung_am_ziel.md`; vom 2026-11-13 bis dahin ging das Paket ins PAKET-LAGER, Plan `docs/plan_paket_lager.md`; das Lager ist am 2026-11-21 gestorben): `GameRun.purchase_card_pack`/`purchase_dice_pack(pack, price, rng)` zahlen, würfeln das Kleingedruckte (`_roll_purchase_refund`) und ÖFFNEN eine Kopie sofort (`_purchase_open` → `_open_pack`); Ergebnis ist die `PackChoice` (null = nichts gebucht). `_open_pack` öffnet mit dem Glück, den Charms und den Klauseln von JETZT: der GLÜCKSWÜRFEL fällt (`_roll_luck_die` → `PackChoice.die_face`/`die_value`), dazu FREIE HAND (das erste GEÖFFNETE Paket der Runde, `packs_opened_this_round`, `_free_hand_bonus` = +1, Winkeladvokat +2 → `free_bonus`), die Optionen sind GENAU `CardPack.option_count(size)` (Beigabe, Beipack, Verplombt und `pack_option_delta` sind am 2026-11-21 gestorben), und die Wahlen sind `_choice_picks` = Augen + Freie Hand, gedeckelt auf die Optionen. **Der GLÜCKSWÜRFEL** (`LuckDie`, 2026-11-21): sechs Seiten ab 1, je Dreiecks-Schwelle des wirkenden Glücks (`effective_luck`) ein Auge auf eine zufällige Seite unter `CAP` 6 (`pips_for`, `next_threshold`, `sync` liefert je Änderung `{face, delta, value_after}`), Rücknahme vom Stapel `gained`, `roll`, `to_dict`/`from_dict` (es gibt noch keinen Spielstand), dazu für Körper und Station `PIP_LAYOUTS`/`pip_layout`, das Kreuz `NET_LAYOUT`/`net_cell` (Seite 0 = oben in Ruhe in der Mitte), `expected()` und `prev_threshold`; `GameRun.luck_station_state()` liefert der GLÜCKS-STATION reine Daten (`luck`, `prev`, `next`, `expected`, `free_bonus`, `keeps`); `GameRun.luck_die`, `sync_luck_die` ist der EINE Schreiber (der `luck`-Setter und `charms_changed` rufen ihn), Signal `luck_die_changed`, Hover `luck_die_hint`; das Güte-Glück (`pack_luck`) füttert nur den INHALT. Zugabe (`pack_pick_bonus`), Glücksgriff (`pack_luck_factor`), Hauptgewinn und `take_all` sind gestorben. Das Ergebnis ist ein `PackChoice` (core/: Art, Paket, Optionen, Wahlen, `rerolled`) in `pending_pack_choices` — Buchung vor dem Licht; das Kleingedruckte erstattet beim KAUF den Paketpreis (`last_card_pack_refund`), das Sammelalbum zahlt beim ÖFFNEN $1 je Paket. `confirm_pack_choice(choice, indices)` bucht die Wahl eines KARTEN-Pakets (über `grant_pack`; eine Würfel-Auswahl weist es ab, Ergebnis `{"cards"}`), Ungewähltes verfällt; WÜRFEL-Pakete bucht `confirm_dice_choice(choice, {Options-Index: Pool-Index})`: es prüft Wahl, Sitz-Bereich und Eindeutigkeit, bucht ALLE Paare atomar per `become` in die Pool-Instanzen (EIN `pool_changed`) und liefert je Paar `{option, option_index, pool_index, replaced}` (`replaced` = Kopie des alten Würfels, für die Zeremonie); ungültig = nichts gebucht, ein leeres Dictionary schließt die Auswahl („Nichts nehmen“) — Paket-Würfel gehen nie mehr durch `pending_dice`; `reshuffle_pack_choice` würfelt die Optionen einmal je Paket neu (1 ⚡, `pack_reshuffle_cost`; Neu gemischt einmal je Runde gratis, `free_reshuffle_used_this_round`). Würfel-Optionen: `DiceOffer.roll_pack_die` (seit dem 2026-11-14, Plan `docs/plan_wuerfelpaket_umbau.md`): jede Sorte zieht JEDE Vorlage und kann Seele, Material und Rune bringen — Seele `pack_soul_chance` (15 % + 3 Punkte je Glück, Deckel 75 %; die Seelen-Regel des Ladens überschreibt die Chance), Material wie im Laden (`roll_refinements`, 35 %, ein bis zwei Seiten), Rune `pack_rune_chance` (15 % + 1,5 Punkte je Glück, Deckel 50 %, eine Rune auf eine freie Seite); die Sorte GARANTIERT nur ihre eigene Eigenschaft: Seelen-Paket immer beseelt und eine Seltenheit gehoben, Material-Paket zwei Material-Seiten (`MATERIAL_MIN_FACES`), Runen-Paket eine Rune und mit der Runen-Chance eine zweite. Veredelung kommt aus Paketen nicht mehr (nur das Gütesiegel). **WÜRFEL-Pakete gehen einen eigenen Weg** (`_roll_dice_choice`, Spec Obsidian-Seite 46): das Glück ist `pack_luck(pack)` = `effective_luck()` + das Glück der GÜTE (`DicePack.quality_luck`, +0/+6/+12) und gilt für ALLE Ketten dieses Pakets — Würfelzahl `DicePack.base_count` (= die Augen, keine Zugabe-Kette mehr) → je Würfel `roll_pack_die` (Seelen- und Runen-Chance mit demselben Glück); die Wahlen sagt der Glückswürfel, und ein Würfel-Paket darf ALLE nehmen. `shop_tier()` (die Augen-Vorlagen) hebt die Güte NICHT. Gebucht in `PackChoice.base_count`/`picks` (`qualities`/`quality_of` sind mit der Güte je Würfel gestorben); `reshuffle_pack_choice` würfelt bei Würfel-Paketen ALLES neu. Laden-Güte über `shop_dice_pack_quality_weights()` (der Haken für künftige Klauseln), Nebenwette `SideBet.reward_dice_pair` = das teuerste bezahlbare Paar aus Augen (2..6) und Güte, Würfel-Paket-Gewinn ab `DICE_PACK_MIN` $10. `effective_luck()` = Glück + Glückspilz (+5 je Kopie) trägt `progress_tier`/`shop_tier` und die Pakete. Preis 0 ist der Wett-Weg: `SideBet.Payout.CARD_PACK`/`DICE_PACK` (je Gewicht 8, ab $9 bzw. $14) — `reward_card_pack`/`reward_dice_pack(factor)` ist das größte Paket ≤ Gewinnwert, `_pay_side_bet` öffnet es über `_award_pack` sofort (`SideBet.awarded_choice`, `from_bet`; `awarded_stored` ist gestorben); gezeigt wird es, sobald der Laden steht. **Das MODUL-PAKET** (2026-11-13, Spec Obsidian-Seite 47; seit dem 2026-11-17 OHNE Wahl) ist die dritte Paket-ART (`LANE_CARDS`/`LANE_DICE`/`LANE_MODULES`, `pack_kind_of(pack)`): `purchase_module_pack` zahlt, würfelt das Kleingedruckte, den GLÜCKSWÜRFEL und die OPTIONEN der Größe (`ModulePack.option_count` 2..6) und legt ALLE über `stash_part(part, true)` nach `pending_parts` (Ergebnis `Array[ComboPart]`, leer = nichts gekauft); wie viele davon EINGEBAUT werden dürfen, bucht es in `module_keeps` (Augen + Freie Hand, gedeckelt; mehrere offene Beutel addieren) und `last_module_roll`. `install_pending_part_at` zählt für Paket-Module herunter; bei 0 verfallen die übrigen (`_lapse_pack_parts` → `last_lapsed_parts`, die Zeremonie liest sie), ohne wartendes Paket-Modul fällt `module_keeps` auf 0 (`_settle_module_keeps`). Der Preis steht in `PRICES` 7/10/18/20/24. `_open_pack` kennt keine Modul-Pakete mehr, `PackChoice.KIND_MODULES` ist gestorben. Eingebaut wird in beliebiger Reihenfolge: `install_pending_part_at(index, key, slot)` (`install_pending_part` = Index 0, für Wett-Module); ein belegter Platz tauscht wie bisher, aber das von einem Paket-Modul VERDRÄNGTE ist verloren; `install_displaces(key, slot, index)`/`install_loses_displaced(index)` liefern der Kopfzeile das Opfer, und `discard_pending_part(index)` verwirft ein NEUES Paket-Modul (nur `from_pack`). Der Einzelkauf im Schwarzmarkt behält seine `parts_room`-Sperre. Laden: ein Platz der Paket-Fläche (seit dem 2026-11-16; davor `SHOP_MODULE_PACK_SLOTS` 1), `module_pack_shop_price` (Supraleiter wie bei Modulen), `shop_module_pack_grade_weights()`; `roll_shop_part`/`purchase_part`/`part_shop_price` sind gestorben. Nebenwetten zahlen weiter EINZELNE Module (`Payout.PART`), kein Modul-Paket. Schwarzmarkt, Automaten, Boss-Prämie, Charms und der Sonderposten-Platz bleiben bei Einzelkarten (`purchase_pack`).

### Die FORTSCHRITTSSTUFE (2026-10-24)
Die gekaufte Hub-Ausbaustufe ist abgeschafft (`hub_level`, Ausbau-Knopf, Stufennamen, Aufstiegs-Prämien und ihre Zeremonie sind fort; Spec: Obsidian-Seite 30). Alles, was sie freischaltete — Blättern, Nebenwetten, alle drei Automaten, Schwarzmarkt —, steht ab Runde 1 offen. Jede Leiter, die mit ihr wuchs (Rarität 6/9/10, Sonderposten ab 8, günstiges Blättern ab 8, Augenwachstum der Würfel, Wett-Unlocks, Automaten-Preise, Serien-Leiter), liest jetzt `GameRun.progress_tier()` = `clampi(1 + luck / LUCK_PER_TIER, 1, TIER_MAX)` (`LUCK_PER_TIER` 3, `TIER_MAX` 10). **Seit dem 2026-11-07 hängt sie am GLÜCK** (`GameRun.luck`, ein Laufzustand ohne Signal): `advance_round` gibt +1 je geschaffter Runde, die Glücks-Klauseln (`DealClause.TAG_LUCK`, alle `Scope.INSTANT`: Glückspfennig +1, Glückssträhne +2, Glückskind +3 — der Winkeladvokat verdoppelt —, Unglückszahl −2) drehen daran, `add_luck` hält es ≥ 0, `next_tier_luck()` nennt die nächste Schwelle. Ohne Glücks-Klauseln ist das byteweise die alte Rundenleiter. `DiceOffer`/`SlotPrize`/`SideBet` bekommen die Zahl als Parameter `tier`. Der Laden-Neuwurf je Runde nimmt die neue Stufe von selbst mit. Die Platzzahlen des Ladens hängen seit dem 2026-10-25 an KEINER Stufe mehr: `SHOP_CHARM_SLOTS` ist feste 3, und seit dem 2026-11-16 liegen alle Pakete auf EINER Fläche zu `SHOP_PACK_PLACES` 6, deren Plätze ihre Art je unabhängig würfeln (`SHOP_PACK_KIND_WEIGHTS` Karten 3 : Würfel 2 : Module 1, `shop_pack_kind_for(roll)`; `shop_dice_slots`/`shop_pack_slots`/`shop_module_pack_slots` sind gestorben), nur die Qualität wächst. `Charm.FEATURE_SECRET_SHOP` steht in `charm_offer_features()` darum immer auf true (Hehlerware ist nie gesperrt); `FEATURE_SLOT_MACHINE` steht seit dem 2026-11-26 auf false, denn die Automaten haben den Tisch verlassen (kein Freispiel im Angebot).

### Quota, Stresstest & Verträge mit dem Haus
The round goal is called **Blind** in all UI text (der Blind; Spieler-Entscheid 2026-11-07 nach Testspielen — Benchmark, Quota und Punkteziel sind gestorben, der Code behält `quota`). Every last round of a goal block (6, 12, …) is a **Boss-Runde** (UI-Wort seit 2026-11-07, Code `stress`; `GameRun.is_stress_round`), and the settlement that ends it is the **Abrechnung**. Surviving it pays a prize: `GameRun.grant_stress_reward()` rolls ONE die with a guaranteed soul and stashes it into `pending_dice`, right after `settle_block_deals` — the ONE `_grant_reward_die`, so the unique/secret exclusions and the soul fallback come from one place. The ceremony `scene_root._play_reward_die_ceremony(die)` reveals the die seal over the hub and flies one comet down the workshop ader into the **Ausgabefach**, where the die rises through the tray floor. Nothing here can fizzle any more — the Magazin's cap does not reach the tray. Booked first, flown afterwards, never awaited.

**Die HITZE (2026-11-28, Plan `docs/plan_hitze.md`; die ETAGEN-KARTE — `floor_plan`, `floor_index`, `floor_moves`, `move_due`, `floor_history`, `move_to`/`move_through`/`walkable_moves`/`move_clauses`/`move_step_factors`/`blind_after_move`/`toll_percent`/`floor_view_state`, `floor_changed`, `BLIND_MOVE_STEP` — ist gestorben):** der Block ist wieder `GOAL_BLOCK` 6 (`is_stress_round(n)`, `block_of_round(n)`, `is_boss_round()`), der Blind wächst in `advance_round` um `blind_growth(n)`, und `advance_round` würfelt die Auslage (`roll_route_offers`) — `new_run` auch schon für Runde 1. `heat` (`HEAT_MIN` 1 .. `HEAT_MAX` 5, Setter klemmt und meldet `heat_changed`). Auslage: Platz 0 `offer_level_for(h, 0)` = H−1 ohne Malus (am Rand H=1 ein zweiter Stufe-1-Deal mit Malus), Platz 1 H, Platz 2 H+1 (bei H=5 `HEAT_WHITE` 6); `offer_heat_delta_for` klemmt die Wahl auf 1..5. `_draw_card(level, cool, treat)` liest `HEAT_RECIPES` (je Stufe Listen von `DealClause.Tier`, Weißglut mit einer BOSS-Kondition unter den Mali), erst Mali, dann Boni ohne deren Tags; `_draw_clause` meidet Auslage, Karte im Bau, Block-Unterschriften, `_quota_blocked`, `_clause_offerable`. Karte: `CARD_LEVEL`, `CARD_HEAT_DELTA`, `CARD_BONUS`/`CARD_MALUS` (Array[String]), `CARD_BOSS`, `CARD_TREAT` (das Werbegeschenk mit `treat_chance()` streicht den Malus des MITTLEREN), `card_clauses(card)`. `take_route(index)` schreibt `block_log[runde]` = {heat: Stufe, pick: `PICK_COOL`/`KEEP`/`HOT`}, bucht die Hitze und unterschreibt; `blind_after_offer(index)` ist dieselbe Rechnung als Vorschau. **Verträge mit dem Haus are the progression system.** Every round the player signs one of three deals (`GameRun.route_offers`, since 2026-11-28 shaped by the HITZE, see above — round 1 included). A contract is **assembled from two clause pools**: one bonus + one malus `DealClause` of the **same tier**, never a fixed pair. **No deal outlives its round** — except through the BLIND and the LUCK: every clause is `Scope.INSTANT` or `Scope.ROUND`, but since 2026-11-07 the INSTANT blind clauses (Blind-Aufschlag +10 %, II +20 %, Blind-Schock +35 %, Wucherklausel +50 % — `GameRun.BLIND_RAISE` —, Eichung −10 % via `CALIBRATION_FACTOR` 0,9, applied `pow(…, deal_bonus_factor())`) multiply `blind_value` at the signature (`raise_blind`) and so lift this round AND every later one; the luck clauses move `luck` for good. `Scope.BLOCK` carries exactly ONE clause, the **Stammkunde** (Ladenpreise −15 % bis zur Abrechnung, `REGULAR_CUSTOMER_FACTOR`); `_scope_reaches` lets it live through every round of its block and `settle_block_deals` ends it. `active_deals` entries still stay in the list **until the Abrechnung** even though their effect is long dead: the entry locks its clause against being offered again inside the block (`_taken_this_block`). `_scope_reaches` is the single arbiter of "is this clause live"; every effect query goes through `_clause_active`, never through raw membership (bonus vs. malus is a property of the clause, so ONE query suffices).

**Die Blind-Kurve** (2026-11-07; `goal_for_round`/`GOAL_INCREMENT` sind gestorben): `blind_value` ist der exakte, ungerundete Akkumulator (Start `BASE_GOAL` 150). `advance_round` merkt erst den WIRKLICH gespielten Blind (`blind_history`, = `effective_goal()` der Runde), gibt +1 Glück, setzt `block_fumbles` beim Blockwechsel zurück und multipliziert dann mit `1 + blind_growth(n)` = 0,10 + 0,05 × `block_of_round(n)` (R2–R6 +10 %, R7–R12 +15 %, …). Gerundet wird NUR das Ziel: `round_goal = floor_to_blind(blind_value)` (abwärts auf `BLIND_ROUNDING` 5, mindestens 5). `projected_blind(k)` rechnet die Kurve ohne künftige Verträge voraus, `roadmap_goal(n)` liefert dem Fahrplan Historie / laufend / Projektion. **Pechsträhne** (Malus T2, `Scope.ROUND`, Tag quota) hebt je echtem Fumble ihrer Runde `blind_value` um 5 % (`note_fumble`, seit 2026-11-09; vorher 2 %); die Boss-Kondition **Strafgebühr** kostet je echtem Fumble $5, nie unter $0 (`last_fumble_fee` für die Anzeige).

The three cards are the three tiers, left to right and **always all three from round 2 on** — **Standardvertrag / Risikovertrag / Knebelvertrag** (the Knebel is no longer gated to block 2). `TREAT_CHANCE` (30 %) turns **one** slot into a **Werbegeschenk**: the slot simply drops its `CARD_MALUS` and draws its ONE bonus out of the **normal bonus pool of its own tier** — there is no dedicated treat pool, no `also_treat` double role, and `Tier.TREAT` survives purely as the offer's *display* tier (card title and `CasinoStyle` colour). Which slot is weighted by `TREAT_TIER_WEIGHTS` (0.6 / 0.3 / 0.1), so the Standard slot usually gives the gift and the Knebel slot only rarely — `_roll_treat_slot` returns that index (−1 = none), and `_draw_card(tier, treat)` is what stamps the display tier while keeping the pool natural. Pairing is guarded four ways: bonus and malus of one card **share no tag** (otherwise the house sells zero-sum nonsense like "Auszahlung ×2 / Auszahlung ×0,5"), no clause lies out twice, no clause already signed this block, and never a second ROUND-scoped blind raise while one is active (`_quota_blocked` reads `QUOTA_MALUS`, which since 2026-11-07 holds only the boss condition Hohe Erwartungen +500 %; permanent raises may stack, that is their point). An exhausted pool falls back to repeats — an empty slot is worse. Effects resolve **only** as `GameRun` queries dispatched on the clause id — never a silent branch in `scene_root`. `effective_goal()` = the stored `round_goal` × the ROUND-scoped factors only (`_round_goal_factor`: Hohe Erwartungen, and the charm Galgenfrist −3 % per real fumble of the round, `blind_value` untouched) — a directly set goal still moves the bar — and `stage_size`/`cumulative_threshold` hang off it, which carries it into the bar, the overcharge stages and the win check at once. ### Die SERIENSCHALTUNG (die Werkstatt seit 2026-09-01)
Der Presse-RNG ist tot. Jede Kassette trägt ein **PRÄGENETZ** (`StampNet`, in `data/`): sechs Zellen, eine je Würfelseite, bei der **Erzeugung** gewürfelt, mit dem Pack serialisiert (`Pack.stamp_net`) und im Laden voll sichtbar — der Zufall lebt nur noch im Nachschub. Je Zelle **genau eines**: Zahl-Bonus, Faktor, Material-Id, Runen-Id, Operator-Id, Veredelung oder Pointer (mit bei Erzeugung gewürfeltem Nachbar-Ziel — er KOPIERT, siehe *Pointer* unten). Die Wurftabellen hängen an Sorte × Größe und stehen alle beieinander (`NUMBER_CELLS`/`NUMBER_VALUES`/`PATTERN_CHANCE`/`JACKPOT_RANGE`, `MATERIAL_CELLS`/`MATERIAL_MONOLITH_CHANCE`, `RUNE_CELLS`) — Balancing ist eine Zahlenänderung. `Pack.tiered` ist der EINE Weg, an dem Name, Preis **und** Netz zugleich wachsen; ein anderswo gesetztes `tier` bliebe mit dem Netz der kleinen Größe stehen.

**`SeriesResolver` ist die ganze Rechnung** — rein, rng-frei, ohne Nodes, und darum ist die Live-Vorschau der Werkbank buchstäblich dieselbe Rechnung wie die Buchung. `resolve(nets, die, terms)` liest die Serie **strikt links → rechts**, je Seite und je Kanal, und liefert EIN Projektions-Dictionary: `bonus` (Array[int], je Seite), `materials`, `doped`, `runes` (je Seite die geänderten Slots), `copies` (je gelungener Pointer-Kopie `{card, from, to}`), `fizzled` (`{card, face, reason}`) und `faces_after` (Endzustand der Augen). Kanalregeln: Zahl-Zellen addieren, ein **Operator** rechnet auf der bis dahin aufgelaufenen SUMME (nie auf dem Seitenwert — ×2 auf einer 6er-Seite wäre absurd), Material **schichtet** (spätere übermalt frühere; zweimal dasselbe = veredelt), Runen: die spätere gewinnt Platz 0, ein Duplikat weicht in einen freien aus. Operatoren wirken **nur im Zahl-Kanal**. v1-Katalog in `StampNet.OPERATORS`: **VERDOPPLER** (×2), **SPIEGEL** (Summe auf die Gegenseite), **SAMMLER** (alle Summen auf diese Zelle) — Sammler VOR Verdoppler ist der Jackpot-Zug, und genau das ist das Puzzle. **Die FAKTOR-Zelle** (`StampNet.KIND_FACTOR`, `{"value": n}`) des MULTIPLIKATOR-NETZES rechnet wie der Verdoppler auf der aufgelaufenen Summe ihrer Seite, nur mit eigenem Faktor und auf mehreren Seiten zugleich — die Grundzahl des Würfels vervielfacht sie nie, und eine Zahl-Karte NACH ihr bleibt unvervielfacht. Das Projektions-Dictionary trägt dazu `factors` (das Produkt der Faktoren je Seite, reine Anzeige: das Summen-Netz zeigt „×4“ auf einer Seite ohne Bonus). Die Treibladung kommt weiterhin NACH allem.

**Die Serienlänge** (seit dem 2026-11-23: `drum_slots`, zwei ab Lauf-Start, je weitere bisherige + 1 ⚡ über `buy_drum_slot`; die Leiter unten ist gestorben, ebenso das unbegrenzte Prägen — EINE Prägung je Runde, `press_allowed`, die Erdungsklemme verbraucht sie nicht) stand auf `GameRun.SERIES_LADDER` (seit 2026-09-04 EINE Sprosse: Fortschrittsstufe 1 → 6), abgefragt über `series_slots()`, das den Sockel um den Einmal-Schub der Nebenwette (`SeriesResolver.BOOST_SLOTS`) ergänzt und bei `SERIES_SLOT_CAP` deckelt (**6** seit Layout E, 2026-11-25, Plan `docs/plan_werkstatt_layout_e.md`: sechs Scheiben, Start 2, Preise 3 … 6 ⚡; Layout D hatte kurz **8**, davor **6** seit der Welle U, der Block selbst); Klauseln drehen seit dem 2026-11-09 nicht mehr daran (Kettentreiber und Kurzschluss sind gestorben).

**Zwei GameRun-Methoden, eine Wahrheit.** `resolve_series(pack_uids, die)` ist die Vorschau und mutiert nichts; `apply_series(pack_uids, die, rng := null)` ist das PRÄGEN und bucht **atomar** — prüfen, rechnen, über `_project` durch die vorhandenen `DieDefinition`-Schreibwege schreiben (`set_face_material`/`dope`/`set_rune`, damit Veredelung, Runen-Plätze und Einbrand-Regel gelten wie überall), Karten verbrauchen, `packs_changed`/`press_changed`/`note_pool_changed`. Das Prägen ist seit dem 2026-09-05 (Welle V) UNBEGRENZT — `press_uses`, `press_allowed()` und `reset_press_cycle()` sind gestorben, es geht mit JEDER Kartenzahl ab EINER (Spieler-Wunsch 2026-10-25), und eine Serie ohne Netz-Karte bucht nichts (`{}`). Es sind **pack_uids, keine Indizes** — Magazin-Indizes brechen beim Umsortieren —, und **ihre Reihenfolge IST die Serie**. Buchung vor dem Licht bleibt Gesetz: `apply_series` bucht sofort, die Zeremonie zeigt nur an. Der Zielwürfel ist ein **Parameter**; die geloste Vierer-Aufspannung (`roll_clamped_dice`, `clamped_dice`, `press_target_allowed`, Signal `clamped_changed`) ist ersatzlos gestorben, ebenso der nasse Guss samt Journal (die Live-Vorschau ersetzt die Rücknahme) und `PhantomPress`/`PressTargeting`.

**Die zwei Katalysatoren belegen weiter einen Serien-Slot als Preis** und werden beim Prägen verbraucht (`catalyst_terms`): **Erdungsklemme** = dieses Prägen verbraucht die Sitzungs-Pressung nicht (wörtlich erhalten, wirkungslos — OFFEN); **Treibladung** = +1 auf jede gefüllte Zahl-Zelle des **fertigen** Summen-Netzes (bewusst am Ende, sonst verdoppelte ein später gestecktere Verdoppler den Zuschlag mit). Die Doppelmatrize ist am 2026-10-25 gestorben (samt `second_die` und `SeriesResolver.secondary`), der TAKTGEBER am 2026-10-26 (Spieler-Entscheid): seit dem harten Deckel 6 hob er nichts mehr, und mit ihm sind `series_slot_bonus`, `CATALYST_TIMER_SLOTS` und `terms["timer"]` gefallen. KEIN Katalysator überlebt mehr die Serie, in der er steckt. Eine Serie aus lauter Katalysatoren prägt nicht. Die **Zwinge** (`CharmEffects.pack_survive_chance`, 25 % je Kopie, Deckel 75 %) würfelt je verbrauchter Karte einmal; eine Überlebende hat trotzdem voll gewirkt und bleibt im Magazin.

**Operator-Kassetten sind Sonderbestand**, neben (nicht statt) Pointer und Veredelung: `Pack.operator_pack(id)`, im Magazin auf `SHELF_SPECIAL`, größenlos, und in `Pack.SPECIAL_ROLL_WEIGHTS` mit Gewicht 10 neben den fünf Regal-Katalysatoren (je 10) und der Gravur-Familie (30). **Das MULTIPLIKATOR-NETZ** dagegen ist REGALWARE: `Pack.multiplier_pack()` ist ein Zahlen-Paket (`TYPE_NUMBER`, Zahlen-Reihe, mit Größe) mit Faktor-Zellen statt Zahl-Zellen (`StampNet.roll_factor`: Standard ×2, Groß ×3 auf 1–2 Seiten, Kolossal ×4 auf 1–3), `$9` vor dem Größen-Faktor; seine Identität IST das Netz (`Pack.is_multiplier()` = `StampNet.is_factor_net`). Es liegt nur im normalen Laden, wo `Pack.roll_engraving_pack(tier, rng, multiplier_chance)` eine gewürfelte Zahlen-Karte mit `GameRun.shop_multiplier_chance()` (4 % + 2 % je Fortschrittsstufe über 1, Deckel 25 %) zum Multiplikator macht — nie im Schwarzmarkt, nie als Wett-Einsatz (`GameRun._stakeable`).

### Extending content
A new **charm**, **engraving**, **essence** or **rune** needs no registration map: add a factory method + `all()` entry in the `data/` class and its effect in the matching `core/` effects class, both wired by the same id constant. Three charms died with the edge purge and must not come back by accident — **Rahmenvergolder** (it became a byte-for-byte duplicate of Goldschmied), **Magnetring** and **Zargenglanz** (no targets left); two more died with the Veredelung, which left no ladder to climb — **Politur** and **Gießkanne**; the **Gravierstift** died with the Verbrauchs-Haushalt (nothing is consumed any more); three more died with their souls in the Essenz-Rework — **Sumpflaterne** (Irrlicht), **Sonnenfinsternis** (Korona) and **Druckerpresse** (Ethylen), and the three essences `will_o_wisp`/`corona`/`ethylene` went with them. Careful with the Politur: the *etching* `Engraving.POLISH` ("+1 auf alle Seiten") shares the id string `"polish"` and lives on. **Asset filename = content id** (`assets/models/<charm_id>.glb`, `assets/textures/engravings/<engraving_id>.jpg`); paths are derived by convention. `DieDefinition` is shared by reference — call `instantiate()` before mutating a die you take ownership of, or a later upgrade mutates every die sharing that definition. **`Engraving.Rarity` drives exactly two things**: display (`rarity_name`, read by the Lexikon) and the **net roll's** material/rune weights (`RARITY_WEIGHTS`/`weight_of`, consumed by `StampNet._pick_material`/`_pick_rune`) — so a rarity edit moves the odds a cassette prints. The rarity-tinted Lichtsaum (`EngravingRenderer.SEAM_COLORS`) died with the Presse. The old floors and single prices died with the Presse; strength comes from the net's size, never from the rarity.


**Essenz-Charms hängen an ihrer Seele.** Every essence from `Rarity.RARE` upwards has exactly **one** charm that sharpens it, and the pairing lives in one table: `Charm.ESSENCE_REQUIREMENT` (charm id → essence id). That table is also the **offer rule** — `Charm.offerable(pool, owned_essence_ids)` is the single gate the shop spread, the Schwarzmarkt charm slot and the slot-machine prize all pass through, so a charm never lies out while its die does not exist (twenty dead cards would dilute an 83-charm pot by a quarter). The charm reads the **effect, not the die**: a Quintessenz that borrows Ozon also borrows the Gewitterfront, because every hook takes `charm_ids` next to the already-resolved essence set. The Quecksilberlampe charm deliberately stays **out** of the table — it belongs to every activation factor, not to one soul. Adding a new essence ≥ RARE therefore means adding its charm too; a test asserts the two lists stay in step.

**A pool entry is never swapped, only overwritten** (`DieDefinition.become`): the round deck, both trays and the workbench grid all hold the *same* instance as `owned_pool[i]`, so a purchase that replaced the instance left every one of them on the old die. `exchange_pending_die` (the Ausgabefach's Tausch — since 2026-08-31 the ONE and ONLY way any die reaches the pool, the slot-machine prize included, und seit dem 2026-09-13 geht der Spieler ihn SOFORT: `pending_dice` ist kein Zwischenlager, sondern die Strecke bis zum erzwungenen Raster — siehe *Der ZWANG* in `scripts/ui/CLAUDE.md`; `_replace_pool_entry` died with the silent write) therefore writes **into** the entry — and every change to a pool die reports it through the single signal `GameRun.pool_changed`: the in-GameRun mutators emit it themselves, outside mutators (engraving station, `MaterialEffects.apply_take_effects`) call `note_pool_changed()`. `scene_root._on_pool_changed` is the one refresh path (three trays + `DiceController.refresh_faces` for the dice lying in the pit + the open station); never patch a single view after a mutation.

**Replacement vs. arrangement.** That rule governs *replacement* only. `GameRun.reorder_pool(from, to)` **moves ONE die**: it is taken out at `from` and inserted at `to`, and everything between rides one place along — deliberately not a swap, so a die lands exactly where it was dropped and the row closes behind it (the same `remove_at`/`insert` the queue tray's `_animate_reorder_move` has always used; a swap would fling the drop target back to the source slot). The instances travel to their new slots with their identity intact and their contents are never rewritten, which is exactly why instance-keyed state (Phosphoreszenz's base and mult stores, all keyed by `get_instance_id()`) stays attached to the right die for free. `become` = a die's contents change; `reorder_pool` = a die's *place* changes. `GameRun.reorder_pool_full(order)` is the same rule for the WHOLE row at once — the Kreislauf am Rundenende, where the pit's content becomes the new pool: it takes only a **permutation of the same instances** (size, no null, no duplicate, nothing foreign) and otherwise leaves the pool untouched rather than half-rewriting it. **The deck IS the pool, and it stays that way**: `_start_new_round` copies `owned_pool` and `_on_pool_changed` re-copies it while the round is still open, so the whole bench window shows the dice where the player put them — and **das Rundenstart-Mischen ist 2026-08-30 gestorben** (Spieler-Entscheid): `scene_root._commit_round` (the signature, or the first throw in a round without offers) leaves the order alone (die Frische Ware, die beseelte Würfel nach vorn zog, ist am 2026-11-09 gestorben). The **only** shuffle left in the game is the Ablage-Reihe of the pit, mixed once when it appears (`DiceTrayView.shuffle_row`), and the round-end order is therefore readable off the table: `reorder_pool_full([Warteschlangen-Rest][ungezogener Rest][Ablage])`. Arranging before the round is now draw-order strategy for the WHOLE round, not just reading strategy. Reordering rides the same window as engraving (`_dice_editing_locked()` false: pre-signature bench time and the shop), and reaches `reorder_pool` from two places — dragging a die onto another in the pool tray (`DiceTrayView.SLOT_PICK_LAYER`, the one hit path) and dragging tile onto tile in the DOSSIER's grid (the Paket-Platzierung was the old second place; the dossier inherited it, and the tap/drag split keeps the two gestures apart). Everywhere the split is the same and needs no arbitration: **dragged rearranges, tapped selects** — a `Button` only emits `pressed` when the release lands on itself, so the reorder and the pick can never both fire. The tray drag therefore also runs **while a Platzierung stands** (only `REORDER_DRAG_THRESHOLD` decides which gesture it was); the press is not a modal state for the trays. The pool tray drag is the queue tray's gesture exactly: the die leaves its slot, a `_spawn_deck_ghost` follows the pointer, and on drop every affected slot becomes a ghost that glides to its new place (`_animate_tray_reorder`, shared `_queue_index_after_move`). Two rules hold it together: the ghosts are registered **before** `reorder_pool` is called, because their presence is what makes the `pool_changed` refresh stand down until the animation ends; and the refill goes through `_refresh_dice_trays`, which picks the source by phase — in the shop the whole pool lies in the tray (`_return_dice_to_pool_tray`), otherwise it is the round deck. `DiceGridView.reorder_enabled` is **off by default**; only the dossier switches it on (the Ausgabefach's old exchange picker, the other user of that class, is gone — its Tausch is a drag on the pool seat now).

### Hand choice & Farkle (fixed rule)
`DiceScoring.best_hand` walks `HAND_PRIORITY` top-down and takes the **first** category that qualifies and isn't throttled — there is **no score comparison across categories**. What physically lies on the table wins, however many points a lower-ranked chip would pay (three Zweierpäsche are never taken as a well-equipped Zwei Paare). Consequence: the throttle fall-through still works (a locked category is skipped, the hand slides one rank down).

**Straßen laufen auf dem Ziffernring 0-9.** A straight of length L is L consecutive positions on the *cyclic* ring of Kombinationsziffern (`_digit` = `value % 10`) — start anywhere, wrap through the 0 allowed, so 8,9,10,21,22,23 (digits 8,9,0,1,2,3) is a large straight and the classic 1-6 straights are unchanged. Digit 0 is a full citizen; beyond 1-6 the ring is only reachable via engraved or grown faces, which is the point. The **Zahnlücke** charm is the one charm that reaches into *detection*: with it, L distinct digits inside a window of L+1 ring positions qualify — exactly one hole. The plain window always wins over the holed one, the rule itself lives in `CharmEffects.straight_gap_allowed`, and `charm_ids` therefore rides through the whole detection chain (`qualifies`/`_qualifies_plain`/`_has_straight_of_length`/`_indices_for_straight`/`_participating_unsorted`/`wild_value`).

The **Farkle rule is the same measure**: `is_strictly_better` compares only the `HAND_PRIORITY` **rank** of the two picked hands (`hand_rank`, smaller = higher) — **points never decide**. A reroll is safe iff it lands a strictly higher rank; the same rank farkles even with far more eyes (pair of 2s → pair of 6s), a lower rank farkles, and 3er-Pasch → 4er-Pasch can never farkle. Materials, edge materials and combo parts stay in the signature (they belong to the hand) but no longer influence the verdict; the **throttle in the ctx still does**, because it decides *which* category `best_hand` picks. A hand that scores nothing at all (parity/throttle) is never progress.

**The last dice are played out.** The round only ends automatically on a full overcharge, the hand cap, or an **empty** pool (`_round_should_end`) — a leftover of 1-5 dice is thrown as a smaller hand (the draw loop already stops at the pool's end). Consequences: `DiceController.set_slot_defs` pads the unused slots (every slot query runs over `count()`), detection runs over the **lying** dice only (`_auto_select_best_combo` goes through `_pit_combination_slots`, or the empty slots' zeros would form their own Pasch), and a hand that can neither be taken nor rerolled is a **Fumble**, not a dead end — `_on_farkle(false)` then skips the forgiving charms/clauses, which would only leave the hand unplayable. Leftover-dice payouts (Sparprämie & Co.) therefore only come from banking early; playing the rest out is the player's choice.

### Scoring trigger order (fixed — NO exceptions)
**Transform charms run first and are ONE ascending chain** (`CharmEffects.transform_value`): 1→6, 2→3, 3→4, 4→5, 5→6, 7/9→8. Ascending by design, so the chain keeps climbing — a 2 reaches the 6 with all four link charms owned — and every result (6 or 8) falls outside the trigger values, which keeps the chain idempotent. The Achterknoten is the only one coming from above (engraved faces climb past 6), and because detection uses `value % 10` it moves the Kombinationsziffer too — that IS the charm. The pit shows the effective value **on the die**: `DiceController.set_value_overrides` paints the up face's number in `DieFaceDisplay.PREVIEW_NUMBER_COLOR` (the same green as the workbench engraving preview — green means "not in the def" everywhere), driven from `scene_root._sync_transform_previews` on every `_refresh_ui`/`pool_changed` and silent during `Phase.SCORING`, where the running value owns the number instead. Display only: `values` stays what the scoring read. The charm side ends in `CharmEffects.shown_by_charms` — chain first, then the Schutzgeld offset (+10 per copy, deliberately a multiple of ten so the Kombinationsziffer stays put), which is a **separate step precisely because it is not idempotent** and must run once per face; the **essence lens runs right after it** and `DiceScoring.shown_value(raw, charms, essences)` is the one function that answers "what does this die show". The preview calls it, not `transform_value`, and `MaterialEffects` asks `shown_by_charms` wherever the take counts face triggers, or simulation and def would disagree on how often a face fired.

There are **two charm classes**, and the class decides *when* a charm fires:

- **Die-bound charms** (`CharmEffects.die_charm_*` hooks) hang on a concrete die and fire **with that die** in the die phase — Mehrfachstecker/Straßenbesen/Bodensatz/Prime Time/Kleinvieh per combination die, Hochstapler with the highest scored die (`die_charm_target_mult_at`), Quadratur with the lowest. `die_charm_crit_at` currently carries **no** charm (Beherit moved to the static crits) and survives as the stage for the next die-bound crit. When a die fires more than once (Argon & Co., Hasenpfote, Echo-Kammer, Nachglühen — `MaterialEffects.die_trigger_count` × `face_trigger_count`), its die-bound charms fire **once per firing**, interleaved: Würfel → Charm-Anteil → Krit-Schlag, then the next firing. Within one firing: eyes → material (`MaterialEffects.*_once`) → die-bound additives → die-bound crits.
- A charm that writes **permanent face values after the hand** is neither: it is a step in `MaterialEffects.apply_take_effects`, and it belongs at the **end of the tail** — after bone/glass, the Miasma spread, Kontrastmittel, so it sees final values and nothing overwrites it, and before the Streulicht loop, which only reads unscored dice. The Gleichrichter is the reference (`_rectify_faces`): protection (`value_protected`) blocks only the **shrink**, a protected die still counts into the mean, and every touched slot goes into `report.grown`/`shrunk`, which is what buys the flares and `note_pool_changed` for free — no scene_root change at all. Nothing here may touch the scoring simulation; the count is over.
- A charm whose effect depends on **what the hand counted before it** is neither, because the `die_charm_*` hooks are stateless: it needs an accumulator threaded through **both** `_base_and_mult` and `ScoreBreakdown.build`. The Wasserfall is the reference (`CharmEffects.cascade_mult` + a `cascade_last` running through the die loop, links included); its contribution rides in the firing's `charm_mult_add` with its dock index merged into `die_charm_indices`, so the pad still flashes at the right moment. The **combination** is the other exception: `CharmEffects.combo_factor` (Dreifacher Boden, ×3 per copy) scales the combination's **base points only** — `points_for` at the call site, never `mult_for`, and both functions are the pure category values.
- **Static charms** (`charm_*_at` hooks) depend on the hand/state, not on a single die. They fire **after all dice, strictly in ownership (dock) order** — additive bonuses AND crits (Einserkult, Snake Eyes, Zwillingsring, Feierabendbier, Galgenhumor, Beherit) apply at their exact position, never pooled at the end, so dock order is gameplay-relevant. The Vorreiter is one of them too — `charm_base_bonus_at` hands it the eye sum of **all lying dice**, once. **Static crits** multiply the *current* mult (`charm_crit_at`, `crit_x` on the charm step) — that hook is the only factor left in the charm phase, and Snake Eyes clamps its ×Augensumme at 1.0 so a sum of 0 or 1 is no crit at all. There is no total-level phase any more: `charm_total_factor_at` died with the old Zauberkarte, and `post_steps` survives as an empty stage in the breakdown.

**Die-phase order is the lined-up row and is scoring-relevant.** The sweep iterates `DiceScoring.trigger_order(scored, dice, declared, participating)`, and the rule is **combination-first**: the combination dice form blocks by Kombinationsziffer, the blocks stand by **size desc, then digit desc**, inside a block by shown value desc and tie → smallest slot; the merely counted dice (Vollzähler, Krypton) fall in behind by the same value/slot rule. A group therefore stays together — a Full House counts 3-3-3-5-5, never 5-5-3-3-3, and a straight reads 6, 5, 14, 13, 2. Without a `participating` list (older callers) there are no blocks and the plain value order remains. `_line_up_settled_dice` does not re-implement any of it: it CALLS `trigger_order` with the selection as the combination, so row and count can never drift. Those values are the **shown** ones everywhere: `hand_shape` feeds `trigger_order` its `shown_values`, and on the table side `scene_root._shown_pit_values()` is the single answer the physical lineup, the take's `trigger_order` and the Flickenteppich's `target_die` all ask — a raw 3 that Fuchsschwanz turns into a 4 lines up, targets and pays as a 4. Because a material or essence ×-crit books inside the die phase, this order *changes totals* by design (mult added after the crit die's step escapes the ×), and the Wasserfall, the Stroboskop and the Miasma spread all read it as a sequence — that's why the order must stay canonical; never derive it from physical positions. `participating_indices` still returns slot-sorted indices; "der zuerst gewertete Würfel" (Echo-Kammer) means the **head of that row** — `hand_shape`'s `echo_slot` is `order[0]`, so a declared arrangement moves it.

**The running value (Knochen/Glas between activations).** When a die fires more than once, bone growth and glass shrink happen **between** the countings — the second activation counts the grown (or shrunken) value. `_base_and_mult` keeps the running values of the **whole hand** (`running_values`, seeded from `raw`) and mutates the firing die's entry after every activation via `MaterialEffects.mutate_value_once`, the **same helper** `apply_take_effects` writes into the defs, so the simulation and the permanent face can never drift (asserted by a test battery over bone/glass/glue/marrow/veredelt × 1-3 activations). The array is hand-wide, not per die, because **Miasma und Radon spread across dice at every firing**: right after the mutation `MaterialEffects.spread_miasma_once` hands the source's halved eyes to every OTHER scored slot, so a die later in the row counts the grown number, the source's own next firing counts its halved one, and with the Weihrauchfass (no self-loss) the same amount goes out every time. It fires on **up-face firings only** — never on a deterministic link, a link being another face; Stickstoff/Einbrand (`MaterialEffects.value_protected`) block the halving and then nobody gets anything. Radon is the **positive twin** (`spread_radon_once`, right behind the Miasma in every one of the three walks): its firing grows the up faces of all OTHER counted dice permanently by +2 (Bleischürze +3) — a GAIN, so no protection blocks it, and the source gives nothing away (its own decay died 2026-11-09). The old silent `foreign_eye_bonus` in the eye sum is gone; the eyes really move now. `ScoreBreakdown.build` mirrors the identical walk, and `apply_take_effects` runs the same spread on the defs — which is exactly why its value-mutation walk iterates in **trigger order** (`_take_order`, the `order` param; a caller without one falls back to slot order) and why the retrigger values are frozen into `_charm_values` before the first mutation: a cross-die spread makes iteration order and mid-walk face reads scoring-relevant, where every other die-local effect was indifferent to both. Three boundaries are load-bearing: the mutation rides the **physical** value (`raw`, the untransformed faces — `score_category` keeps it, `best_hand` therefore passes RAW dice down and only detects on transformed ones), so a transform charm stays a lens and the run ends where the def ends; **eyes, the die's own material math and the AMOUNTS of the die-bound charms** follow the running value (`value_override` = the firing's `shown`, so the Hochstapler adds +4 then +6 on a growing bone face), while `trigger_order`, hand detection, `participating`/`scored` and every charm hook's **targeting** (`target_die`, the row head) stay pinned to the settled **shown** values — charm transform chain plus essence lens applied, never the raw faces and never the mid-count running value: a count must never reorder or retarget itself mid-play, and a transformed die is a transformed die for every purpose but the def; and **links keep their stored values**, a link being a different face. `ScoreBreakdown` mirrors it and gives each activation entry `value` (what it counted) and `value_after` (the physical value after the mutation), which is what `scene_root._play_die_pulse` switches the die's number to between pulses — in the NORMAL color, because the change is permanent, and the def carries the same number once `apply_take_effects` has run after the ceremony.

**Ties on a single-die charm (fixed rule).** When a charm means exactly *one* die picked by its face value (Hochstapler = highest, Beherit = lowest, Flickenteppich = highest), a tie is broken by **the first matching die — the smallest slot**. Never scan for the extreme value yourself; call `CharmEffects.target_die(values, candidates, highest)`, the single source of truth — and hand it the **shown** values, so a raw 3 and a natural 4 tie under Fuchsschwanz and the smallest slot wins.

**Zwei Auslöse-Achsen (fixed).** How often a die's up face fires is **die_triggers × face_triggers** (`MaterialEffects.die_trigger_count` × `face_trigger_count`, product in `total_trigger_count`). Each axis is internally **additive**, the two **multiply**, and there is no third axis. Nothing CAPS them any more either — the Sternschnuppe fires like every other die and only its ×4 crit is rationed (once per round, at the very first firing of its first scoring; the Meteorit moves that to the first firing of EVERY scoring). The **Würfel-Achse** = essence factor (the only factor anywhere) + Echo-Kammer copies on `echo_slot` + Sauerstoff/Sonnenwind order extras + Sechserpack copies once the hand uses all six dice (`CharmEffects.full_hand_retriggers`, fed by the `scored_count` param that **every** `die_trigger_count`/`total_trigger_count` call site must pass — the scored set's size, `order.size()` where the caller has the row) + Zauberkarte copies on the **combination dice** of the round's **first taken hand** (`CharmEffects.first_hand_retriggers`, which owns BOTH conditions so no call site half-rebuilds the rule; the two facts travel as `is_first_hand`/`in_combination` on `die_trigger_count`, derived from the ctx's `CTX_HANDS_TAKEN == 0` and from `participating` — not from `scored`, so a die that only the Vollzähler or Krypton pulls into the count gets nothing. `apply_take_effects` therefore takes a separate `combination` array next to its (scored) `participating`, empty = the two are the same, which is what keeps simulation, take and gold pre-pass byte-identical); the **Seiten-Achse** = 1 + Nachglühen runes + the retrigger charms (Hasenpfote 6 / Kleeblatt 4, on the *transformed* value). So Quecksilberdampf × Nachglühen is 6 firings, not 4. `DiceScoring._base_and_mult` runs them nested (outer die trigger, inner face trigger); each inner iteration is one full firing — eyes → material → die-bound charms → crits → `MaterialEffects.mutate_value_once` on the running value. `apply_take_effects` iterates the SAME nesting, which is what keeps simulation and permanent def byte-identical.

**Pointer** (named so since 2026-08-13 — it was "Leiterbahn"; the table's LED adern keep that word). **Since 2026-10-30 it is no die mechanic any more** (Spieler-Entscheide, Obsidian-Seite 34): `DieDefinition.pointers`, the rolled chance, the link chains, the frozen `CTX_POINTER_FIRES` roll, the arrows and traces on the die, the essences Plasma/Glasfaser and the charms Zündspule/Rückkopplung/Erdungskabel are all dead. It lives ONLY as a Prägenetz cell (`StampNet.KIND_POINTER`, `{"to": neighbour}`, target rolled at creation) and acts ONLY in the SERIENSCHALTUNG: **a Pointer on face A with target B COPIES the series state of A onto B** — the running sum `bonus[A]` (factors already in it), the material the SERIES laid on A (with its Veredelung), the runes the series set on A (`_rune_changes`), and a Veredelung the series put on A even on the die's own material; never the base pips and never a material the die carried before. The copy runs as synthetic cells through the existing channel rules (`SeriesResolver._apply_cell` — Einbrand blocks overpainting, the same material twice dopes, a duplicate rune dodges), A keeps everything, and every channel A does not carry is left alone on B. In the number channel it OVERWRITES (`bonus[B] = bonus[A]`) — unless the **Lötkolben** (`Charm.SOLDERING_IRON`, RARE) sits in the dock: then the sum is ADDED (`terms["pointer_add"]`, the one source `GameRun.series_terms(catalysts)`, read by `resolve_series`, `apply_series`, `WorkshopView._sum_terms` and the Übertragung's `_burn_terms`; it only touches numbers). **All Pointers of ONE card read the state BEFORE that card** (a snapshot at the card's start) and write AFTER its other cells, in face order — no chains inside a card; across floors they chain, and that is the puzzle. Only neighbours (`DieDefinition.adjacent_faces`/`can_point` survive as pure geometry, else `FIZZLE_POINTER`); a source that carries nothing yet verpufft with `FIZZLE_EMPTY`. Pointers cannot be compressed (`compress` treats them like operators — `FIZZLE_OPERATOR` —, `compressible` refuses, `GameRun._compress_parts` reports the blocker `"pointer"`).

**Deterministic links** — a link is one firing like any other with the face swapped in (link eyes → the link face's material via `MaterialEffects.*_once_for` → die-bound charms including ×crits, at the die's position in trigger order, never retriggered, never a rune, never in hand detection) — and there are exactly **two** paths, split by their timing. **Röntgenlicht** (opposite face) comes from `EssenceEffects.essence_link_faces(die, up_face, essence_ids)` and fires **once per die trigger**, right behind that trigger's firing batch — no chance, no chain and never a rune; it rides `CTX_ESSENCE_LINKS`, and `DiceScoring.essence_link_groups` is what rolls its value forward over the triggers (a bone link grows between them). The **Kehrseite rune** (opposite face) comes from `EssenceEffects.link_faces(die, up_face, rune_ids)` and keeps the old once-after-all-triggers path on `CTX_DET_LINKS`. Both are resolved once in `scene_root._link_map` (`_essence_links`/`_det_links`), remapped in `_score_ctx_for_slots`, snapshotted as `pre_reroll_essence_links`/`pre_reroll_det_links` for `is_strictly_better`'s `old_ctx`, and resolved by take effects straight from the defs. A Röntgen die that also carries a Kehrseite fires its opposite face per trigger **and** once at the end — deliberately no hidden dedupe. All three walks (`_base_and_mult`, `ScoreBreakdown.build`, `apply_take_effects` incl. the gold pre-pass) run the same nesting, and the Manometer repeats both link kinds like every other soul effect.
Applied in the Werkstatt: the EPIC dice-category engraving **"Pointer"** (one of the two Sonderposten, next to the Veredelung: `material_id()` returns `""` for every `is_special_id`, so neither is ever rolled into a net and both reach the player only as the fixed content of their own pack) travels as a **Pointer cell** in a Prägenetz; the card nets (`PressNetView.stamp_net`) draw it as an arrow on the source cell's border matching the crossed cube edge (`DieNetView.net_pointer`/`POINTER_SIDES`). The SUM net shows only the RESULT (no arrow) and names a copied face "kopiert von Seite N" (`PressNetView.copied_from`). The deterministic links keep their pulses: `_play_die_pulse` while the pit net re-shows the die with the link face gold-framed.

**Veredelung (normal vs. veredelt).** The player-facing word is **Veredelung** — every UI text and every German comment says "veredelt"; the code keeps `dope`/`doped`/`levels`/`Engraving.DOPING`, the same UI-vs-code split as Energie/`energy` and Gravur/`Engraving`. (It was called "Dotierung" until the material theme won.) `DieDefinition.levels` holds, per face, the state of the material *sitting on that face* — **0 = none, 1 = normal, 2 = veredelt**, and there is no third rung: `DieMaterial.MAX_LEVEL` is 2, so every `level >= MAX_LEVEL` check reads "is this face doped". The state belongs to the material **instance**: `set_face_material` is the single write path for `materials[face]` precisely so that overpainting always resets it to undoped (Midashandschuh, the material engraving and both die factories all go through it) — but it never touches the face's **rune**: die Veredelung wohnt in der Glasur, Runen in der Struktur der Schale. **Die Veredelung is a Sonderposten, like the Pointer** (`Engraving.DOPING`, EPIC, `CATEGORY_MATERIAL`): bought in the Schwarzmarkt, bought in the **normal shop from Fortschrittsstufe 8** (see *Sonderposten im Handel*), won at the Nebenwette Reinraum (`SideBet` `clean_room`, the one `Payout.SPECIAL` besides the Platinenauftrag), sealed as a Fixinhalt-Paket, pressed like everything else, then placed BY HAND on a clamp net. It is deliberately a **material** engraving that belongs to no material: `material_id()` returns `""` for every `is_special_id`, so no net roll can ever produce one — but its sealed pack is a Material-Paket and its Ablage chip glows in the Material sort colour, because saturating a glaze is what it does. It travels as a **Veredelungs-Zelle** in a Prägenetz; a naked face has nothing to dope and a doped one nothing left to gain, so the cell verpufft on both (`SeriesResolver.FIZZLE_NAKED`/`FIZZLE_DOPED`, and the preview names it). The Einbrand bars only *overpainting*, never the glaze on top. `GameRun.apply_series` books it through `die.dope(face)`, which IS the legality probe — a face that does not move is not a target, so the piece survives a misclick. The two born-or-copied sources stay: the **Gütesiegel** charm dopes a face on the dice it refines (`DiceOffer`/`Pack`),, and the SERIES itself: the same material printed TWICE on the same face projects veredelt — the planbare second way. `DieDefinition.dope` stays the single write path. Fresh paint out of the press always lands **undoped** — the saturation is its own piece, never a rung on the material engraving. The old duplicate climb is **gone** — the same material engraving again on the same face is **not a target at all** (the face dims like any other non-target) and fresh paint costs exactly one copy. Each material's doped state is hand-set — sometimes scaling, sometimes transformation: Bernstein +20 flat **plus the eye sum**, doped only 5 × eye sum; Gold +$3, doped +$7 plus +$1 per gold-**face** trigger of that take (counted in one pre-pass before the first booking); Knochen grows +2 flat, doped max(10, 20 %) **compounding per activation**; Rubin +4 Mult, doped a **crit ×2**; Glas +eyes **capped at 6**, doped a **crit ×(eyes/2)** instead of any additive share — and it eats itself −1 undoped, half when doped. Both crit materials wait for the doping, and no material crit ever drops below ×1. The two doped crits are the reason the whole thing is order-sensitive: a material crit fires inside the die phase, in the crit sub-step **before** the essence crit and any die-bound charm crit, in both the activation and the link block — so `MaterialEffects.mult_bonus` can no longer express a full result and survives as an additive preview helper only, with `DiceScoring._base_and_mult` authoritative. Charms that raise a base number keep their **surplus above it** (Bernsteinzimmer +30 in both states, Knochenleim +3 over the step, Goldschmied +$3/Goldader over the state's rate — Knochenmark doesn't touch the step, it adds a **trigger**: every bone firing happens twice, flat, and the doped percentage step recomputes per trigger, so two triggers on 40 give +10 then +11, never 2 × +10) — never a factor on the raised value, or the doping would multiply with its own support charms. The per-die facts the effects need (up-face state, eye sum) ride in `ctx` like the links (`DiceScoring.CTX_MATERIAL_LEVELS`, slot-keyed — **remapped** in `_score_ctx_for_slots` and snapshotted as `pre_reroll_levels`, or preview and farkle comparison silently judge the wrong dice); take effects read the defs directly. `DieNetView.level_badges` marks a doped cell in its bottom-right corner — free, because the card nets' pointer arrows sit at the edge midpoints — as geometry, not type: at the 30-die grid's ~17 px cell a glyph is mush, so the dark plate with its three bars carries alone. The undoped face stays unmarked, it is the normal case. The station composes its own cells, so it adds the badges itself.

**Essenzen (die Seele des Würfels).** `DieDefinition.essence_id` is **innate**: sealed into the shell at casting, with no application path anywhere — only die factories and offers write it (`DiceOffer.roll_essence`, shared by shop, packs and the slot-machine die prize). `Essence` (data) carries name, `short`, `description`, rarity, `glow`, `secret` (Schwarzmarkt-only) and `unique` (Legendäre) — no epithet: every display site prints name + effect; `EssenceEffects` (core) resolves the behaviour by id. **Kanten-Materialien are gone entirely** — the edges are no longer an upgrade slot, only the stage the essence glows on (`DieFaceDisplay` tints the frame by `Essence.glow_for`, essence-less dice keep the neutral neon). Consequences that must hold: **the essence is the only factor on the Würfel-Achse** (`activation_factor` — Argon 2, Quecksilberdampf 3, Elmsfeuer 2/4 im Stresstest, and the Quecksilberlampe **charm** adds +1 to any factor ≥ 2), and there are exactly **two axes** (see *Zwei Auslöse-Achsen* above), so `Faktor × Faktor` can never explode again. `secret` essences never appear in the normal shop or in packs, `unique` ones are excluded once owned and never roll onto a bundle > 1, and **nothing writes straight into the pool any more**: every die (shop, Schwarzmarkt, Stresstest prize, slot machine) is stashed into `pending_dice` and only the player's own Tausch overwrites a slot, so which soul is painted over is his decision, never a roll. The essences ride the ctx as `DiceScoring.CTX_ESSENCES` (slot → id), `CTX_ESSENCE_SET`, `CTX_RUNES`, `CTX_PHOSPHOR_STORE` (slot → stored base points; run-long state only `GameRun` knows — it accumulates and is never emptied) `CTX_PHOSPHOR_MULT` (the same store's mult half, only filled by the Leuchtstoffröhre) plus the hand-wide `CTX_STRESS`, `CTX_ROUND_TRIGGERS` and `CTX_ROUND_CRITS` (what the round's earlier hands brought — only Dunkelkammer and Gewitterfront read them); every slot-keyed one is remapped in `_score_ctx_for_slots` and snapshotted as `pre_reroll_*`. A **third shape of repetition** sits next to the two axes and must not be confused with them: `EssenceEffects.essence_repeat_count` (Manometer) repeats what the SOUL does, never what the die does — its crit (each repeat its own slam, Härteofen grammar), its money per firing, its face growth and its deterministic links. Lenses, Antimaterie's inversion, Krypton's always-scored, protections and every charm-/material-owned effect stay single. **No essence touches the trigger order** — `trigger_order` knows only the canonical combination-block rule (see *Die-phase order*) and the player's declared arrangement.

**Krypton widens the scored set** — and that is *all* it does. It joins `hand_shape`'s `scored` even when it is not part of the combination (`EssenceEffects.always_scored_of`, appended after the legal filter and re-sorted) — but never `participating`, so detection, the combo charms (Snake Eyes, Zwillingsring) and the Zauberkarte's extra die trigger never see it. That is the Vollzähler distinction, and the append must go onto a **copy**: without the Vollzähler, `scored_indices` hands back the very same array as `participating`. The append iterates the **legal** slots, which is what makes a parity-barred Krypton stay barred (see *Parität*). `scene_root._hand_slots` pulls a lying Krypton die into the hand even unselected, otherwise the scoring would never be handed the die it is supposed to always count.

Three shapes an essence effect can take, and each has exactly one seat: a **lens on the shown value** (Wasserstoff ×2) sits in `DiceScoring.shown_values`/`shown_value`, right after the charm transform chain — so detection, targeting and the pit preview all see the doubled number while `raw` (the physical faces, and with them the value mutation and `score_category`) stays untouched; **link faces** (Röntgenlicht's opposite, once per die trigger) come from `EssenceEffects.essence_link_faces`, which the scoring ctx, the take effects and the gold-trigger pre-pass all call — the Kehrseite rune keeps its own once-path in `link_faces`; and **permanent face growth** (Helium, Strahlungsdruck) rides `MaterialEffects.mutate_value_once` for the up face while the take writes the remaining five, so simulation and def can never drift. An essence that pays at the **round's end instead of inside a hand** is a fourth shape and stays a pure helper: Knallgas' chain reaction is `EssenceEffects.leftover_die_payouts(souls_in_deck_order, base, charms)`, which returns the payout **per leftover die** and which `scene_root._on_round_complete` uses in place of the old scalar `per_die` — Leergut/Blackout still kill the whole row, and with it the chain. An effect that reaches into the **run's stock instead of the score** is a fifth shape and does NOT belong in `core/`: the effects class only carries the predicate, the effect itself is a `GameRun` method called from the take, next to `apply_carbon_copy`/`apply_glaze_brush`. Per-round die marks are keyed by `get_instance_id()` and cleared in `roll_essence_round_state`.

**Krits are floats.** `EssenceEffects.crit_once_for`/`crit_of`, `MaterialEffects.mult_crit_once_for` and both `CharmEffects` crit hooks return `float`, and the mult side of `DiceScoring._base_and_mult` is a float all the way down. There is **exactly one rounding in the whole formula** — `ceili(base × mult)` at the merge — so two Xenon crits are ×2.25, never twice-rounded ×2. The crit *count* (Ozon's ramp, Grubengas's per-crit bonus) therefore compares with `is_equal_approx(crit, 1.0)`, never `!= 1`. Every printed mult goes through the one format rule `ScoreBreakdown.format_mult`/`format_number` ("×2", "×1.5", "×2.25"); the combo chips keep printing their integer level mult.

**Runen (Kernlicht).** A rune is a sign etched so deep into the shell that it taps the die's core-light spark — the tint IS the effect. `DieDefinition.runes` holds one per face; `second_runes`/`third_runes` are parallel arrays that **only Vakuum dice** may write (`rune_slots()`, the third only under the Glasglocke charm), and the Vakuum's runes render **black** whatever the type. Five kinds in three classes: **Nachglühen** (Wertung) adds +1 activation, additive like every non-essence source; **Funkenflug** (Wertung) mints +1 ⚡ once per Zug — the fourth energy source, booked in the take and flown afterwards; **Kehrseite** (Wertung) fires the opposite face once after all die triggers, deterministically — the same face the Röntgenlicht lights, only at the face instead of the soul and once instead of per trigger (see *Deterministic links*); **Streulicht** (Ökonomie) pays +$1 (Stichel: $2) for every die that lies **unscored** at the end of the Zug showing that face (the deliberate counter-event to Gold, hence `apply_take_effects` takes the `lying` slots); **Einbrand** (Schutz) burns the face value in — it cannot shrink (glass skips it) and its material cannot be overpainted, while doping that same material, value engravings and re-etching stay allowed. Runes belong to the **up face**, so links and Kehrseite firings never fire them — that rule is what stops two opposing Kehrseiten from escalating into each other. They travel as `DiceScoring.CTX_RUNES` (slot → id list), remapped and snapshotted as `pre_reroll_runes` — the Nachglühen changes activation counts, so the farkle comparison needs the old runes. Acquisition is the **Runen-Paket** through the Presse: its Prägenetz carries 1 / 2 / 3 rune cells by size, and a cell places its rune **once** — the later cell wins the first slot, a duplicate dodges into a free second one (Vakuum) and otherwise changes nothing.

**DIE GRUNDFORMEN: je Rune EINE geschlossene Grundform um die Seitenmitte, die Ziffer DAVOR** (2026-10-24 — der KRANZ vom 2026-09-11 ist gefallen, mit ihm `DIGIT_KEEPOUT`/`digit_clearance`, `SLOT_FLIPS` und der Ziffern-Wächter; die ANKERZELLE schon davor, samt `ANCHOR_CELLS`/`NET_CELLS`/`cell_to_face`/`cell_to_net` und den Shader-Uniforms `cell`/`impact`). The geometry still has ONE source (`Rune.glyph_lines`, normalized polylines) feeding both the net view and the generated 3D overlay texture, written in **face coordinates (0..1 over the whole face)**: Ring, Dreieck, Quadrat, Stern und Raute auf demselben `GLYPH_RADIUS` (0,44). `GLYPH_MARGIN` (0,06) ist kein Zierrat: er trägt `RuneTextures.FIELD` (0,055), also endet der Ausbruch genau am Seitenrand statt dort als Rechteck abgeschnitten zu werden. **Mehrere Runen einer Seite liegen INEINANDER** (`SLOT_SCALES`/`slot_scale`/`place`, im Shader `glyph_scale`) — eine symmetrische Grundform gespiegelt wäre sie selbst (siehe *DIE GRUNDFORMEN* in `scripts/dice/CLAUDE.md`). Das **Würfelnetz** benutzt dieselbe EINE Funktion `place` über die ganze Kachel. `RuneTextures` bakes one colourless mask per glyph (12–43 ms each at `SIZE` 256), and the stroke has **constant depth** (`STROKE`) — die Auslaufzone der freien Enden ist mit den geschlossenen Figuren gestorben. Timing separates the two light systems: the essence glows **constantly**, a rune only shimmers at rest and **flares in the moment it fires** (`DieFaceDisplay.flare_runes`, driven from `_flash_scoring_die`). `IDLE_CEILING` ist seit dem 2026-09-11 nur noch die Bloom-Schwelle des Renderers, and there are no per-rune lights — the tiled ground's 16-light budget forbids it, so emission carries alone.

`ScoreBreakdown.build` mirrors the formula 1:1: every die step carries one group per Würfel-Trigger (`die_triggers`, each `{firings, links}` — a firing is die pulse → `charm_*_add/after` → the `crit_steps`) plus the trailing `det_links`, played by `scene_root._play_die_step`. **The combination travels pure**: `combo` carries the plain `points_for`/`mult_for`, and each Dreifacher-Boden copy is its own `combo_factor_step` right behind it (dock indices so the pad flashes, plus `base_after`/`mult_after`; only `base_after` moves, the combination's mult stays pure) — same rule as the crits, one step per copy instead of one `×3^n`. Folding the factor into `combo` silently made the counter show a number whose origin was nowhere on the table. The sums are unchanged by construction and a test nails them against `DiceScoring.score_category`; the charm has no `charm_*_at` hook, so it can never appear in `charm_steps` as well. **Every crit is its own slam**: `crit_steps` lists material, essence and then EACH die-bound charm position separately (`_crit_step`), so a doped Rubin under the Härteofen hits twice ×2 instead of once ×4 — `crit_x` survives only as their product for the tests; static charm steps stay in the charm phase (`_play_crit_step` for static crits, Beherit among them). When adding a charm, put it in the class where it belongs — a die-bound effect in the `die_charm_*` hooks, a static one in the `charm_*` hooks — or the animation fires it in the wrong phase.

The same rule holds at **round end**: after the round payout and before the shop opens, round-end charms fire strictly left to right in `scene_root._play_round_end_charm_ceremony`, and every effect is **visible** — money charms (Zinsgroschen, Überflieger — $5 per **cleared overcharge stage**, i.e. the visible bar, so the Doppellader's second ⚡ per stage changes nothing —, Glücksgroschen, Notgroschen) pay as **real chip denominations** (`ChipStackView.split_gain`, each packet in its chip color, landing at the head of its groove in the chip tray) launched in a dense stagger from the dock pad, every packet booking its own value on arrival; the Dynamo books its ⚡ and lets the light fly afterwards; Frankiermaschine mints `GameRun.STAMP_PACKS` (1) sealed Pakete per copy and flies them down the workshop ader (`STAMP_METEOR_GAP` between launches, no waiting for the previous arrival), each granting on its own arrival (`GameRun.roll_stamp_pack` rolls the sort, the ceremony grants); the **Füllhorn** pays off the same bar the Überflieger reads — from `GameRun.ENCORE_STAGES` (5) cleared overcharge stages on, `apply_encore(stages)` books one sealed Sonderposten per copy at `_on_round_complete` and `_play_encore_meteor` flies it to its Magazin spot, where its withheld cassette rises out of the pit floor (`_fly_pack_to_magazine`, the one Magazin arrival; the round end IS the once-per-round rule, no flag); the **Schmuckkästchen** grants **Fixinhalt-Material-Pakete, never a face** — it was booked back at `_on_round_complete` (`apply_jewelry_box` returns one `{material_id, copy, pack}` per find; `copy` names the dock seat it belongs to, so several charm copies do not all fire at the first pad) and shows it here as the same dense volley, each meteor ending on its own Magazin spot. All of it travels the LED-strip route through score window, pit and hub (see *Light routing*). A new round-end charm gets its case in that ceremony — never a silent mutation in `GameRun`.

### Das LABOR (2026-11-26, Plan `docs/plan_labor.md` Welle 1)
`Labor` (core/, RefCounted) hält die Stations-Ids `SEELE`/`CHARM`/`MODUL`/`KARTEN` (`STATIONS`, `NAMES`, `TEXTS`), die Regeln `wait_for(value)` = 6 − Augen (eine 6 ist sofort fertig) und `next_station(current, rng)` (nie dieselbe) und den AUFTRAG als Daten (`station`, `inputs`, `die_face`, `die_value`, `rounds_left`, `done`, dazu die Dinge selbst als Referenzen `dice`/`charm`/`part`/`packs`; `to_dict`/`from_dict` sichern nur den Datensatz). In `GameRun`: `labor_station` ("" = zu), `labor_job`, `labor_uses`, Signal `labor_changed`. Die erste Station fällt in `grant_stress_reward` (`open_labor`), `labor_open()` = `stress_tests_passed >= 1` und eine Station. Einsatz-Form: seele `{"dice": [Quelle, Ziel]}` (Pool-Indizes, die Quelle trägt eine Seele, die das Ziel nicht schon hat), charm `{"charm": Index}`, modul `{"part": {"key", "slot"}}`, karten `{"packs": [uid …]}` (1–6, nie Rohling/Verdichtete, Katalysator, Operator — `labor_card_valid`). `start_labor_job(inputs, rng)` prüft (`can_start_labor`), zahlt `labor_price()` = `labor_uses` ⚡, wirft den Glückswürfel über `_luck_roll`/`_luck_value` (Klauseln ja, Güte-Glück nein); `advance_round` zählt herunter; `collect_labor(rng)` bucht (Seele überschreibt das Ziel, Charm-Kopie per `duplicate` über `_grant_charm` — ohne freien Projektor ist `labor_collectable()` falsch und der Auftrag bleibt fertig stehen —, Modul-Kopie über `stash_part`, je Karte eine neue derselben Sorte über `CardPack.roll_contents(…, 1)` mit der Güte, deren Güte-Glück `effective_luck() + 10` erreicht) und würfelt die nächste Station. **Gesperrt, nicht entfernt**: Labor-Würfel bleiben in `owned_pool` (`die_in_labor`, `labor_dice`, eine Hand zieht aus `playable_pool()`), `exchange_pending_die`, `confirm_dice_choice`, `apply_series` lassen sie aus, `reorder_pool` legt um sie herum um (`_order_around`), und `reorder_pool_full` nimmt den Stapel mit oder ohne sie; der Labor-Charm fällt aus `_resolved_charms` und `_neighbor_id` wie `dark_charm` (`labor_charm_slot`), ist nicht verkäuflich, nicht verschiebbar, nicht versteigert und nicht von der Strafgebühr getroffen; das Labor-Modul ist `is_part_disabled` (fehlt in `scoring_parts`), nicht umsteckbar, nicht verdrängbar, nicht vom Fumble-Verlust getroffen (`part_in_labor`, `labor_part_socket`); Labor-Karten verlassen `owned_packs`. `labor_state()` liefert der Ansicht alles als reine Daten.
