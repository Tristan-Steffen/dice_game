# scripts/data/ — Resource-Records

Ausgelagert aus der Wurzel-CLAUDE.md (2026-08-24, verlustfreies Umsortieren). Die Wurzel trägt Projekt, Commands, Schichtenregel und die globalen Invarianten; Querverweise („siehe *X*") können in einer Schwesterdatei liegen (scripts/table/, scripts/ui/, scripts/core/, scripts/data/, scripts/dice/, test/).

- **`data/`** — `Resource` records only: `DieDefinition` (per-die faces/materials/runes + the innate `essence_id`), `Charm`, `Engraving`, `Pack` (sealed shop goods — Gravuren only, a die is never a pack; also home of the Magazin taxonomy — `SHELF_ORDER`/`shelf_of`/`pack_belongs`, four shelves since the Würfel-Fach went —, of `pack_uid`, the identity `GameRun._stash_pack` stamps on entry into `owned_packs`, of the three **Paketgrößen** `TIER_NORMAL/GROSS/KOLOSSAL` with their names, price factors and shelf weights, and of the four **Katalysatoren** (`CATALYSTS`, `catalyst(id)`) plus the one weight table of the Sonderposten-Wurf (`SPECIAL_ROLL_WEIGHTS`)), `DieMaterial`, `Essence`, `Rune`, `RoundEvent` (per-block round events). Each type's ids are `const` string constants (single source of truth) so a typo is a compile error, not a silent no-op that never matches in a `match`. The layer rule bites here: `Rune.tint` **mirrors the `CasinoStyle.CHARGE` literal** instead of importing it (data/ never reaches into ui/), and a test asserts the two stay equal so drift fails loudly.
